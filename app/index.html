About keys

Keys live only in this browser. Back them up: export them and keep the text somewhere safe. Export all carries this actor's keys and everything this browser keeps with them (Rescue: Streetcat's keys, campaigns, donations, requests, partners, colonies, photos, and this browser's refund keys; Partner: the clinic keys and its requests, records and photos); Import all adds this actor's part to what the other browser has, and leaves out another actor's. A public key is what you publish; a private key is what signs. A new or imported key never deletes the one before. Keys are per browser and per address: another browser, a private tab or an app's built-in browser has its own.
