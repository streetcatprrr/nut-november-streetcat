# The app

Two static pages, no server: `index.html` (the tool) and `status.html` (the public status page), both on `cashu-bundle.js` (cashu-ts 4.13 bundled with esbuild from `lib-entry.mjs`:
`npx esbuild lib-entry.mjs --bundle --format=iife --global-name=cashu --minify --outfile=cashu-bundle.js`). `index.html` also loads `qrcode.min.js` for the invoice QR (qrcode-generator 2.0.4 by Kazuhiko Arase, MIT; minified with esbuild, licence header kept), and `catid.js`, the cat recognition, which runs `catid-worker.js` on the files in `ai/1/` and `ai/2/` (121 MB, fetched only after a tap): ONNX Runtime Web 1.30.0 (MIT), YOLOS-tiny and DINOv2 small, quantised to 8 bits (Apache 2.0), and, for the cat's face (version 2, 7 Nov), AnyFace (MIT) and PetFace (its weights for non-commercial research only), quantised to 8 bits too. `ai/1/LICENSES.txt` and `ai/2/LICENSES.txt` name each file with its source, revision and SHA-256. Those files never change; a new version goes in a new folder.

`index.html` is laid out like a wallet: one thing per screen, the primary button at the bottom, a lock bar (locked → certified → released) on every token, the amount as the hero with a € estimate (rate from mempool.space, cached ten minutes), a token card instead of raw `cashuB…` for the clinic and Streetcat, a home screen for Streetcat listing the campaigns made in this browser, and one moment of motion when a payment lands. The header is the three roles — Donor, Rescue and Partner (the clinic, a shop or another); Rescue is Streetcat's role, named for any animal rescue, while the texts inside still say Streetcat and its address stays `/streetcat` — with the one on screen in its colour (its keys' colour), and, apart, a magnifier icon for the status page (labelled Status). Each role sees only its tabs; a role in the header switches to it, and the role on screen, tapped again, goes back to its first screen. On any other screen a framed ← at the header's left goes back: to the screen above it (`BACK`) when that belongs to the same role, otherwise to the role's first screen. The phone's own back does the same: every screen opened is a step in the browser's history (`history.pushState`), so back returns to the previous screen instead of leaving the page. The role is kept in the browser; a first visit opens on Donate, and a campaign link always opens Donate. The status page has the same header, Status marked, and a role there opens the app as that role. Each role also has its own address that shows only that role, with no switching: `/donate`, `/clinic`, `/streetcat` (rewrites in `vercel.json`; locally `index.html?role=clinic`), and `/status` for the status page. Both pages can be added to a phone's home screen as an app (`manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`): the clinic gets a link to `/clinic` and installs it. Tabs:

- **Rescue: what Streetcat pays for** (7 Nov) — Home shows four kinds two by two (sterilisation, vet care, food, other), each with its pots, its requests and what is left to spend; each kind has its own screen. A campaign is a pot of one kind, for one colony or every colony, with the partner who confirms the spending (a clinic, the shop that sells the feed, or another): kind, colony, partner and what the partner is (`type`, `colony`, `partner`, `pkind`) are signed with the campaign (`core.js`), optional, and change neither its id nor its ledger. "Choose colony", "Choose partner" and "Choose pot" each open a list with "+ New". A request is paid only from a pot of its kind, for that colony or every colony; the pot brings its partner and the partner's key, and the cost takes the pot's donations, the oldest first. Partners and colonies are kept in this browser (`vetlock.sc.partners`, `vetlock.sc.colonies`). Two pots of one partner made the same day get different locks (the second refunds a second earlier), and a key that is not a point of the curve is refused (`pointOk`).
- **Partner: confirm** (7 Nov) — the header's "Partner" (inside, the role is still `clinic`). What the pot says the partner is decides its words: a clinic confirms a procedure on a cat as before; a shop a delivery ("Confirm delivery", for which colony, what), with a photo of the delivery note and of the goods, at least one; another partner what was delivered or done. The photos are made small on the phone, named by SHA-256 in the signed confirmation (`statement.photos`, slots `note` and `goods`) and sent to Streetcat by relay in parts encrypted to its key (`streetcat.answer-photo`); Rescue shows them under the request before it releases.
- **Donate** — open the campaign link, choose an amount and pay the **Lightning invoice** from any wallet (QR, "open in wallet", copy). Under Pay with Lightning, as big as it and one under another: Pay with ecash, Take my sats back and Check for requests (7 Nov), with no line of text under them. **Check for requests** asks the relays for the campaigns Streetcat has announced and lists the open ones where the campaign shows: the latest on top, and under it, in a box like the one Keys types its keys into, every earlier one, newest first, its name in its own colour (a tap opens it as its link would) and what it is for in small letters, in two yellows (9 Nov). Newest first by when each campaign was made: its announcement says so, signed with it. Only a campaign signed with Streetcat's published key, named by the announcement and signed by its ledger key, and still open, is listed; no relay reached is said in red. The page asks the campaign's mint for the invoice (NUT-04) and, once it is paid, mints the sats directly as proofs locked to *Streetcat + clinic* (2 signatures), with a refund path, after the deadline, to a key generated in your browser — a new key for every donation. The invoice and its key are kept in the browser, so a reload (a phone switching to the wallet app) picks the payment up again. Or Pay with ecash: a Cashu wallet pays the page's payment request (NUT-18; "open in wallet" hands it to the phone's Cashu wallet as a `cashu:` link, which no Lightning-only wallet opens), or paste a plain ecash token from the campaign's mint, and the page swaps it into the same lock. Either way you get a receipt (keep it private: it holds that key; the browser keeps a copy) and the locked token to send to Streetcat (copy or share). Closed campaigns refuse donations, and so does a link not signed with Streetcat's key, by every route (9 Nov: anyone could take the signature off a link they changed): it says so in red and greys out the three ways to pay; receipts of donations made through links from before links were signed still refund. In the iPhone's home-screen app, which opens no link from outside, Donate opens with nothing chosen whatever its address carries (an icon made from a donation link keeps that link), unless an invoice of the same session is being paid.
- **Clinic: certify** — the clinic's key lives in its browser; it checks the token names that key (or an older one kept there), signs offline, and hands back a *certified* token. If the token names no key held in this browser, it says which keys the token is locked to. It refuses to sign after the deadline.
- **Streetcat: release** — adds Streetcat's signature and swaps at the mint; the mint refuses unless the clinic's signature is there. Without the Streetcat key the token names, the page stops before asking the mint.
- **Donor: refund** — after the deadline, the receipt alone takes the sats back (or just the locked token, from the browser that made the donation: it lists them). Optionally paste a Lightning invoice and the refund is paid straight to it (NUT-05); the page checks the invoice with the mint first, and whatever the invoice did not ask for comes back as ecash.
- **Streetcat: new campaign** — first reads the mint (NUT-06 info, NUT-02 keysets): it needs NUT-11, NUT-07 and NUT-12, says whether NUT-04 Lightning invoices work and what the input fee is. Then builds the donation link for one operation (name, mint, both public keys, deadline, optionally where donors send the token) and the empty ledger for its status page, and announces the campaign on the relays for donors' Check for requests (kind 30078, `d` = "announce:" and the campaign's id, so it never replaces the ledger; signed with the campaign's ledger key; it expires at the deadline). If no relay takes it, it says so in red, and Home has "announce to donors".
- **Clinic: home** — its requests (to review, done), then five buttons of one size, full width, one under another: Check for requests, Open a request, Offline setup (what the phone needs to confirm without coverage; in red while something is missing), Cat records, Settings ("Certify a token", which signed a pasted token with no request, is gone since 12 Nov: the partner signs from a request only). An empty list of cats says nothing. With two requests listed it all fits an iPhone 14–16 screen; with more, the page scrolls a little.
- **Requests: the cat as caught** — making a request, Streetcat can add up to three photos of the cat (face and both sides), taken as it is caught and made small on the phone like the record's. The request names them by SHA-256 (`photos`, optional; an older app ignores them). They follow it by relay, in parts encrypted to the clinic's key (`streetcat.request-photo`; each part names that key, so a part that comes before its request is kept), and Streetcat's request shows when they have gone ("its 3 photos sent"). The clinic sees them on the request, under "Photos from Streetcat, when caught", to check it is the right cat before confirming; a tap shows one big. In person, by QR or link, there is no room for them: the request comes first ("on their way"), and they arrive by relay once the clinic has a connection, without unticking its box. A request saved as a file carries them.
- **Requests: is it the cat?** — once the clinic's record of the cat is on Streetcat's phone (signed with the campaign's clinic key, naming the request), Streetcat's request shows the record's photos under its own, view by view ("When caught · your photos", "At the clinic, asleep · MOCHI's record"), to compare them by eye, with a link to the record. The cat recognition of "Already in the records?" adds a hint, one-sided: under a score of 0.40 it says in red "These may not be the same cat. Look at them, and at the chip, before releasing.", and so does the request's line; otherwise "nothing points to another cat", never "the same cat". The score is the mean, over the request's photos (face, sides), of each one's closest photo in the record. Calibrated on the evaluation's photos, each cat's split into a request and a record: with 2–3 photos a side, the right cat was warned of 0–5 % of the time, another cat 73–80 %, one of the same coat 51–62 %; with one photo in the record the right cat 15–19 %, so it needs two ("Too few photos in the record to compare them here"). Cats of one coat can look alike to it: two orange kittens scored higher (0.78) than one of them against its own other photos (0.69). The first time it waits for a tap (121 MB, as at the clinic); then it compares by itself, and the result is kept with the request. The test photos were of cats awake, and a record's are of a cat asleep: the line is to be checked again with the first real cats. Since version 2 (7 Nov, the face too) the score is on another scale: each pair of photos is compared by DINOv2 and by PetFace, each cosine is put on one scale (how far above the usual cosine of two different cats it is, in their spread) and the two are averaged, and the line is 1.0 on it (`CATID.WARN`); a result kept from version 1 is worked out again. On 54 of the test photos (13 cats), with 2–3 photos a side and the same false alarm for the right cat (5 %), it warns of another cat 84–89 % of the time (version 1: 66–76 %) and of one of the same coat 64–71 % (45–56 %).
- **Requests: deadlines** (plan step 1, 9 Nov) — under the pot, New request says apart: until when the donations are needed (`Required until 17 Nov: the work on 14 Nov, 1 day to confirm and a 2-day margin (estimates)`: the end of the work's day in Madrid, plus the partner's days to confirm — 1 for a clinic or a shop, 2 for another, or as its record says — plus Rescue's margin, 2 days unless changed under "edit"); whether the donations' refunds open after that, read from each proof's own lock (`✓ Refund window opens after the required date (8 Dec)`, or in red: before the required date, before the work, or open already); the funding (`✓ Funding checked at the mint at 14:02`, one question to the mint for the whole pot; with no answer, `✗ Funding not checked · Last verified at 14:02`, from what it said last, and the request still goes, saying so); and what the pot's other open requests hold (this phone only). Before the work the button says `Send anyway`, next to `Change the date` and `Pay from <pot> · refunds from <day>` (only pots of the same kind, colony and partner whose refunds open after the required date and that hold it). With refunds open and no answer from the mint, it does not send. A request not checked at the mint when sent carries `funding: { checked: false, at }`, outside what the partner signs (the format stays `v: 1`; an older app ignores it): the partner's app shows it in red, and asks the mint itself (`✓ Checked at the mint now: 12 of 12 still locked`). Home marks a confirmed request whose refunds open soon (`✗ Release soon: refunds open 10 Nov`) or are open (`✗ Refunds are open: release now`). Donate says when the invoice expires, read from the invoice itself (BOLT 11), and when the mint's quote does only if it differs.
- **Requests: repeat and drafts** (plan step 2, 9 Nov) — under a request's steps, in any state, `Repeat request` opens New request with its kind, colony, its pot if still open and it fits (if not: `✗ TNR MARINA closed on 8 Dec. Choose another pot.`, and with none open, `No open pot for food in Marina · Make a campaign`) and what it is; above the form, `A new request, from R-93DAF649D0 (2 Nov). Check every line before sending.` The cat, photos, cost, date, note and donations are chosen again (under the cost, `Last time: 12 000 sat on 2 Nov · check the cost`), the deadlines are checked again, and it is sent as any other, with a new id: the new one says `Repeated from R-…`, and the old one is not touched. A new request offers `Repeat a recent one`, the last three of its kind that differ in colony, partner or what. `Keep as draft` keeps the form's fields only (`vetlock.sc.drafts`: never proofs, signatures or photos) on its kind's screen, `Draft · 12 sacks`; a draft reserves nothing; opened, it is back in the form, with `Delete draft`; sent, it is a draft no more.
- **Clinic: cat record** — with the cat still asleep, the clinic writes its record: name, colony, microchip (15 digits), sex, ear tip, coat, the procedure and its date, notes, and up to four photos (face, left side, right side, the nose up close). Each photo is made small on the phone: JPEG, at most 640 px and 38 kB, drawn anew so nothing the camera wrote in the file goes with it (place, phone, time). Opened from a request ("MOCHI's record: photos, chip"), it starts from the request's cat, procedure and date and names the request and its campaign. It is short: what is known already is one line with "edit" (the request's cat, procedure and date; without a request, the procedure of the cat before, or sterilisation, and today), coat and notes are folded, and a photo tile opens the phone's camera straight away (`capture="environment"`; a computer offers its files). The record is signed with the clinic's statement key (`streetcat/cat-record/v1`, core.js `signCat`) and names each photo by its SHA-256; it goes to Streetcat by itself, encrypted: the record through the outbox like an answer, the photos (kept in IndexedDB, too big for the outbox in localStorage) in parts of at most 26 000 characters straight to the relays, each part again until two relays have it. Or "save as file": the record and its photos in one. Nothing in it is public.
- **Clinic: Check the cat** (7 Nov) — on a record made from Streetcat's request, Streetcat's photos as caught show at once under the clinic's own, and the one button compares the clinic's photos with both: with the request (the hint of "Requests: is it the cat?", one-sided, before signing: "These may not be the cat of Streetcat's request", in red) and with the records, as below.
- **Clinic: Already in the records?** — once the new record has a photo of the face or a side, and the clinic has records with such photos, the new photos are compared on the phone with theirs. The three records that look most like it are shown in order, each with its photo most like the new one, its name, colony and date; a tap opens one, and ← comes back to the new record as it was. It is only an order, never "the same cat". In the evaluation (58 photos of 14 named cats from Wikimedia Commons, kept out of the repository), the cat's own record came first 66 % of the time and among the first three 97 %, but no score told a cat already recorded from a new one, so the app shows the order and nothing more: "Only an order, not a match: open one to compare the chip". How it works: YOLOS-tiny finds the cat and the photo is cut to it (10 % margin, a padded square); DINOv2 small turns it into 384 numbers; a record's score is the mean, over the new photos, of each one's closest photo in the record (cosine). The photos are resized as Pillow does it, so every browser gives the same numbers. Since version 2 (7 Nov) the face counts too: AnyFace finds the cat's face and five points on it (eyes, nose, mouth corners), the face is turned and scaled onto PetFace's cat template, and PetFace turns it into 512 numbers; a face counts only from a score of 0.6, and with no face in either photo DINOv2 decides alone. On the test photos the right cat came first 89 % of the time, against 63 % with DINOv2 alone. The first time, it asks before the 121 MB come, then shows them coming; they come from the app's own site, and the service worker keeps them, apart from the app (`streetcat-ai-1`), for use without coverage. A donor's phone never downloads them. Each photo's numbers are worked out once and kept in IndexedDB (`streetcat-catid`), under its SHA-256. Nothing leaves the phone. If the recognition cannot come, it says so in red, with the button to try again. Offline setup has a line for it: ⚠ until it is saved, with a button to get it ahead of time, on wifi, showing the megabytes come. It saves the files into the service worker's copy without starting the recognition, and it is not needed to be "Ready for offline use".
- **Requests: sent by themselves** (7 Nov) — the request, the clinic's answer, the records and the photos go by relay as soon as they are made (the outbox sends them again until two relays have them), and each says so: "✓ sent · 2 of 3 relays have it", then "received by the clinic" or "✓ received by Streetcat" when the other phone's receipt comes back. The link, file and QR buttons show only when it has not gone (no connection, the browser cannot reach relays, or every relay refused it), under a red "✗ Not sent…"; once it has gone, a small link ("Clinic offline? Show it as QR", "Streetcat offline? Show it as QR") still shows it as a QR for the other phone in person.
- **Nothing received is lost without a word** (8 Nov) — the outbox keeps a message's copy only while it waits; once sent it keeps how it went (the last 300), so the app's storage no longer fills with copies of photos already delivered. A donation, answer or record Rescue's app cannot take for a reason that can pass (no room left on the phone, no answer from the mint) is said in red in the check's line and asked for again at each check; one that is wrong in itself (not this campaign's lock, a mint signature that does not hold) is said in red for a week; a donation sent for a campaign whose Streetcat key is not in this app is said in red too. Once, the first check of this version reads again from the relays every donation the campaign's ledger does not hold, so those lost before come back. A donation sent while the app is still sending something else goes at once, after it.
- **Relays slow to confirm** (8 Nov) — a relay that was sent a message and gave no answer in time may have taken it all the same (nostr.mom confirmed 20–30 s late that day, nos.lol never): it is asked for it by its id before it counts as not taken. An announcement or a ledger is asked about again 20 s and 45 s later, and its line follows: "✓ Announced to donors: 1 of 2 relays have it", in red each relay that does not have it and why ("nos.lol: no answer"), in grey those still asked; with none yet, "announcing it to donors: no relay has confirmed it yet…", not in red. At each new try the outbox first asks the relays that were sent it and gave no answer, and does not send it again to one that has it. One announcement of a campaign at a time. The default relays: nos.lol, relay.primal.net, nostr.mom, nostr.oxtr.dev and purplerelay.com.
- **Rescue's Home without the Lightning address** (9 Nov) — Home has the two numbers, the four kinds and the buttons, with no field and no line over them. In Keys, as Rescue, a long button "Lightning address" under Export all / Import all opens the field; the address is checked when it is first shown and when it is saved, and only what is wrong is said, in red (no "✓ Every release is paid at once to…" there either). Releases are paid to it as before; the Partner's Keys has no such button.
- **The Partner's home: two numbers, its requests behind them** (9 Nov) — entering Partner shows only "pending" (all not done: to review, refund date passed, waiting to be sent, reported) and "done", then the buttons. A tap on a number shows its requests, typed in, the number underlined in Partner's blue; a tap again hides them. Back from a request the home shows what it showed; entering Partner again starts with none shown.
- **The donor starts with nothing chosen when the app is opened again** (9 Nov) — a campaign picked from Check for requests goes in the address marked `&pick=1` and is remembered for the session; in a new session the mark is dropped and Donate starts empty. An invoice left unpaid waits below ("Unfinished Lightning donation · resume") and is still finished by itself if it is paid; it opens by itself only on a reload in the same session (back from the wallet app). A link opened from outside opens its campaign as always.
- **The donation goes to Streetcat by itself** (8 Nov) — as soon as a donation is locked (Lightning, pasted ecash, or a Cashu wallet's payment), the donor's app sends the locked token to Streetcat by relay, through the outbox, with no button. Where "send it to Streetcat" was, a line says how it goes: "sent to Streetcat ✓", or in red while no relay has it ("no relay took it yet · trying again by itself", "offline: it goes when there is a connection"), and it is tried again while the app is open. A donation already on its way is not sent twice. After a day with no relay, or with no room to save it, a tap sends it again. "copy token" and "hand it over in person" stay for a phone with no connection; a donation handed over in person after it went by relay shows "in person" in Rescue's campaign, with the volunteer's receipt.
- **Donate starts again from whatever it shows** (8 Nov) — while Donate shows a donation made, an invoice waiting to be paid or Streetcat's open requests, the header's arrow shows; it, the phone's back, the role in the header and "donate again" go back to Donate as it starts. An invoice skipped stays below ("resume") and is looked at each minute: paid after all, the donation is locked and shown; expired unpaid, it goes (nothing was charged). The list of open requests closes when Donate is left.
- **Cats** — the clinic's records, with whether each has gone, and Streetcat's, as they arrive from the relays (or from a file): searchable by name, chip digits, colony or coat, each with its photos and what the clinic wrote. Streetcat takes a record by relay only from the clinic key of one of its campaigns, keeps a photo only when its SHA-256 is the one the record names, and lists a record signed by any other clinic key (from a file) as "unknown clinic", in red.
- **Hold to confirm** (7 Nov) — every button that moves money or signs (Take my sats back, Lock my donation, Confirm procedure, Send the report, Sign and send to Streetcat, Sign and release, Release, Make the request, Make the campaign, Accept and sign) needs holding for two seconds: it fills from the left in the colour of whose screen it is (donor orange, Rescue green, clinic blue), blinks twice, buzzes where the phone allows it, and acts. A tap alone does nothing and says "hold to confirm"; let go early and the fill empties. Space or Enter held does the same; a long press opens no menu. Pay with Lightning only shows an invoice (the wallet is where it is paid), so it stays a tap. `vetlock.holdms` in a browser's storage sets the time (the tests set 0: a tap).
- **Keys** — generate the browser keys; **Export all** / **Import all** move this actor's keys and everything the browser keeps for it (Rescue: campaigns, donations, requests, partners, colonies, photos, this browser's refund keys; Partner: its requests, records and photos) to another browser: what that one has stays, and another actor's part is left out and said. Everywhere a key is shown it is painted in the colour of its role — orange (Bitcoin's) for donors' refund keys, blue for the clinic, green for Streetcat — with each group of 4 characters in its own shade taken from the key itself, so the same key always looks the same and two keys of one role can be told apart at a glance. A new or imported clinic or Streetcat key never deletes the one before: it is kept in the browser and still signs the tokens locked to it, and exports carry it. Keys are per browser and per address: another browser, a private tab or an app's built-in browser keeps its own.
- **A payment from a Cashu wallet** (NUT-18; 15 Nov) — a payment that comes to a request is written down with it first (under the PIN with the request's key), then taken; one that fails in a way that may pass (the mint, or its info, out of reach; no room) is tried again, from 5 s up to every ten minutes, and never dropped. Every payment that came is taken, and the app's own check looks at the open requests whatever screen it shows. A request stays open until its campaign's deadline. A second payment to a request already paid is a donation of its own, under a refund key of its own; ecash already locked with the request's key joins the first payment in one token. The same proof twice is refused. Ecash locked with the dest rule is taken only if the mint says now that it enforces the rule (otherwise it is kept for its refund, and a volunteer or Rescue does not take it), and its DLEQ is checked against each proof's own keyset. The messages are read page by page from each relay (relay.js), and what is not a payment is not opened again.
- **What an interruption leaves** (15 Nov, six scans, group 4). The mint must say it restores (NUT-09): no campaign is made at one that does not, and no donation goes through it. A swap's record (`vetlock.inflight`) stays while its ecash is spent and the mint's restore gives nothing yet. `vetlock.pending`, the Lightning invoices waiting, is one of the places a PIN seals. A dest invoice paid after its mint stopped saying it enforces the rule is minted as plain ecash for its donor (`renderTakenBack`), never as a donation. A payout's "paid" mark is put again as the app opens (`reconcilePayouts`), and change that could not be kept is owed (`changeDue`) and asked back from the mint, once (`notHeld`).
- **Releases and collections** (15 Nov, six scans, group 5). `C.destProven` (core.js): a transaction under the dest rule is this request's only when the witness the mint keeps with its first input names these inputs, opens these outputs and carries Streetcat's signature and the partner's; `spentByTx` (Rescue) and `cCollect` (the partner) ask it before a payment is booked or sent on. `ledgerPart` writes a release's part once for each request. `releaseInputs` (donations of before 15 Nov) finishes a release found done at the mint. A request's amounts check is bound to the request (`qDigest`, `amounts.for`). A request not kept is neither acknowledged nor sent; one refused whole by the mint lets go of its ecash (`freed`). The partner's collection looks at old pots once an hour, and at every request when an address is saved.
- **One tab at a time** (15 Nov, `onetab.js`) — the app keeps its records in the browser (localStorage, IndexedDB), and two of its tabs open at once wrote over each other: of 120 records kept at once by two tabs, 8 to 23 were lost, released ecash among them, and a PIN removed in one tab while the other was being opened with it left the keys sealed under no PIN (the six scans of 14–15 Nov, twelve findings). So a browser runs the app in one tab: the first takes it (a Web Lock, let go of when that tab closes), and another says "Open in another tab", runs nothing of the app and writes nothing, and opens it with "Open it here" once the first is closed, never by itself (the first, opening again after a PIN or an update, finds it free). The vault writes only in the tab that runs the app; the status page opens next to it and only reads. A tab the browser keeps aside (back/forward cache) lets go and opens again from the start. A browser without Web Locks runs the app as before.

Every call that creates ecash (minting a paid invoice, and the swaps that lock pasted ecash, release and refund) builds its outputs in the page and saves them in the browser *before* asking the mint; if the browser cannot save them (its storage full), nothing is sent. If the answer is lost, the page asks the mint for it again (NUT-09 restore): at once, or the next time it opens. A swap's record stays until what came back is kept where it goes (the released token, the refund, the locked donation): if the phone's storage refuses it, the page says so in red, shows it to copy, and asks the mint for it again each time it opens. Released tokens are kept in the browser too, until Streetcat moves them to its wallet, and all of them are listed. Nothing that holds money or a key (released ecash not moved yet, payouts and refunds still waiting for the mint, payment requests still waiting) is dropped for being old or many; the writes that come before a payment are checked, and a refused one stops it, in red (review of 9 Nov). A campaign link is checked before any money moves: a whole-number deadline in the next two years, valid and distinct keys, an https mint; after minting or swapping, the page checks the lock written in the new proofs. `STREETCAT_KEYS` (one line in `core.js`) pins Streetcat's published key (`0294637dac…`).

## Status page

`status.html?l=<ledger URL>` (or paste the ledger). A ledger is one JSON file per campaign, published by Streetcat in `app/campaigns/` — for example `app/campaigns/mochi.json`, opened as `status.html?l=campaigns/mochi.json`. Only `app/` is published, so the ledgers live inside it:

```json
{
  "campaign": { "name": "MOCHI", "op": "sterilisation", "mint": "https://mint.example", "streetcat": "02…", "clinic": "03…", "deadline": 1796083199 },
```

`donations` holds every locked token exactly as donors sent it. Publishing them is safe: they need the clinic's signature, or after the deadline the donor's own refund key, and each donation has its own refund key.

For every donation the page checks, in the browser and against the mint only:

1. **The lock** written in each proof: Streetcat + clinic, 2 signatures, the campaign deadline, a refund key; signed proof by proof (`SIG_INPUTS`, donations made before 15 Nov) or whole (`SIG_ALL`, every one since).
2. **That the mint issued it**: the DLEQ proof (NUT-12) against the mint's published keys. `UNSPENT` alone proves nothing — a mint says that about anything it has never seen.
3. **What happened**: the proof state (NUT-07) and, for spent proofs, the witness the mint accepted. *Locked* / *refundable* (deadline passed, unspent) / *released* (clinic and Streetcat signed) / *refunded* (the donor's refund key signed). For a donation signed whole the signatures are on the whole transaction, so they are checked over the text the spend's witness gives back (`core.js` wholeSpends); a spend whose witness gives nothing back (a refund made with another wallet) is *spent · not proven*, and counted as nothing released.

Opened in the browser that made donations (same address as the donation app), the page also lists **the donations made from this browser**, from the app's own copies of the locked tokens, with the same checks against the mint; the campaign is read from the lock in each token (Streetcat's key first, as the app writes it). "Copy them as ledgers" gives one ledger per campaign, ready for `app/campaigns/`; refund keys are never in it.

Tokens that fail 1 or 2 are listed as *not counted*, with the reason. A box lets any donor check that their own token is listed. What the page cannot prove: that the list is complete (an omitted donation stays locked and refundable), or that the mint still holds the bitcoin behind its ecash.

Open both pages locally with any static server (`python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app`) — a `file://` URL also works for `index.html`, since the bundle is a classic script; `status.html` needs a server to fetch a ledger by URL, but a pasted ledger works anywhere.
